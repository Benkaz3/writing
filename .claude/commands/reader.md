Give a reader report on `$ARGUMENTS`. The first word is the slug under content/posts/. An optional `vN` names the version (default: the latest).

Follow the "Reader report" section of CLAUDE.md in this repo. Steps:

1. Read content/posts/<slug>/_index.md for `genre:` and coach/lenses/<genre>.md so you know what reader you are.
2. Read the named version once, as that reader.
3. Reply in plain prose, under 12 lines, no headings: where attention held, where it slipped and what was on the page at that moment, and one question you were left with. No flags, no suggestions, no rewrites, no praise, and no closing remark about what you logged.
4. Append a short "reader" entry to coach/log/<slug>.md. Touch nothing under content/.
