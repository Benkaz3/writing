This is a place to practise writing and thinking in public.

Each piece is written in versions. Version 1 is the first honest draft. Each later version is my own revision, made after a short round of feedback that points at no more than three things and never rewrites a sentence for me. The "what changed" note under each version is written by me, in my own words, so the record shows what I learned and not just what changed.
